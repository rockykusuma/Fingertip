Point at it.

A big pointing hand for presenting on your Mac.

Fingertip puts a hand where your cursor is so everyone watching knows exactly what you mean. Draw, add arrows, highlight, spotlight part of the screen and number your steps, then get out of the way.

Everything is one shortcut away

Fingertip lives in the menu bar. Right-click its hand for the menu and Settings.

ShortcutWhat it does
⌃⌥HPointer on or off. Clicks become taps of the hand, so nothing underneath is pressed.
drag ⇧ drag ⌥ dragDraw, draw a straight arrow, or highlight. Ink fades after a few seconds.
⌘ clickClick through: the app underneath gets a normal click, and a ripple shows it.
⌃⌥LNext pointer style: hand, laser dot, or lock-on brackets.
⌃⌥SSpotlight: dim everything except a circle around the pointer.
⌃⌥ASnap to buttons, menus and links, with a label naming what you point at.
⌃⌥NStep stamps: each tap drops the next numbered badge.
⌃⌥X escClear drawing and stamps. Esc also turns the pointer off.

On your screen

Made for showing, not just telling

“Click the thing in the top right” never works on a call. Fingertip makes the thing obvious, then fades away so the next point is just as clear.

⌃⌥L

Three pointers

A hand in the style of the menu bar icon, a red laser dot, or lock-on brackets that clamp onto whatever you point at.

⇧ · ⌥

Draw, arrows, highlights

Drag to draw. Hold Shift for a straight arrow, Option for a see-through highlighter. Press harder on a Force Touch trackpad for a thicker line.

⌃⌥A

Snaps to the real button

Uses macOS Accessibility to find the button, menu item or link under the hand, centres on it and shows its name.

⌃⌥N

Numbered steps

Each tap drops 1, 2, 3 on the buttons you press, so a walkthrough turns into a ready-made tutorial screenshot.

HOLD ⌘

Point, then click for real

Plain clicks only tap the hand, so you never press something by accident. Hold ⌘ when you want the app to get the click.

⌃⌥S

Spotlight

Dims the whole screen except a circle around the pointer, for when the audience needs to look at one thing.

MULTI-DISPLAY

Every screen

Follows your cursor across all your displays, including full-screen apps and every Space.

PRIVATE

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account, no analytics, no network requests. Privacy policy.

Settings

Make the hand yours

Every choice applies instantly and is remembered.

  • The menu bar icon's hand, the same hand in six skin tones, or Apple's ☝️
  • Finger on the left or right, or Auto so the palm faces the nearer edge
  • Hand size, laser, bracket, ink, highlighter and stamp colours
  • How long ink stays, spotlight size and darkness
  • Open at login, tap sound, hide the system cursor

Get Fingertip

  1. Download the DMG, open it and drag Fingertip to Applications.
  2. Fingertip isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch shows a warning. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Click the hand in the menu bar or press ⌃⌥H, and allow Accessibility when asked. That's what turns a click into a tap of the hand.
