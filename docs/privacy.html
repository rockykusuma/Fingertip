Fingertip for Mac

Privacy policy

Fingertip collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests at all.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings, such as the hand, colours and sizes, are stored on your Mac.
  • What you draw, highlight and stamp exists only on screen and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Accessibility access

Fingertip asks for Accessibility access for two things. It turns your clicks into taps of the hand while the pointer is on, and it reads the name and position of the button, menu item or link under the pointer so it can snap to it and label it. This happens on your Mac, only while the pointer is on, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. You can remove the access any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, GitHub and Google can see your IP address. The site uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

Children

Fingertip collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
